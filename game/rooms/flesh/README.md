# Flesh: design brief

Inside something alive. The tightest corridors in the game opening into the
second most open rooms. The contrast is the point.

- **Corridors: veins.** Tightest of the five biomes, super cramped. Long,
  narrow, winding, 1 wide for stretches. Walking a vein should feel like the
  party is being squeezed through.
- **Rooms: massive open spaces.** Second most open, behind the dungeon. A vein
  should open into a chamber that is big and clear, so the relief of arriving
  is real and the fight that follows has room.
- **Floor.** Flesh floor slows movement. Smooth stone strips are fast lanes.
- **Doorways: 1 wide.**

## Where the current rooms stand (2026-09-22)

- The corridors and maze pieces already feel like veins.
- The big rooms are big in outline but chopped up inside: Stomach is 106 floor
  tiles with only 17 clear ones, Lung is 71 with 9. They need clear centres to
  be the open chambers this brief wants.
- Ribcage, Chambers, Closet and Closes are small and single file. Fine as veins,
  not as chambers.
