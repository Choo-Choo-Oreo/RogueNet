# Forest: design brief

Woods, water and glades. Good as it is: the current set is the reference for
what a party-sized room looks like.

- **Corridors: middle of the pack.** Trails are mostly 2 wide, with brambles
  and water shaping the route.
- **Rooms: middle of the pack.** Glades and marshes are open, thickets are
  broken up. Water is difficult terrain, so ponds and streams shape a fight
  without blocking it.
- **Walls.** `wall_forest` is the dense treeline. `barrier_forest_dense` is the
  impassable version for the edge of the world once barriers are in.
- **Doorways: 1 wide.**
