# Dungeon: design brief

Built masonry. Corridors are the loosest in the game and the rooms are the most
open. The dungeon should feel a bit cramped, but not very cramped: the
tightness comes from the 1-wide doorways, not from the corridors.

- **Corridors: 3 wide.** Loosest of the five biomes. A party of six walks
  abreast, and a fight can spill into the corridor.
- **Rooms: a 3-wide feeling everywhere.** Most open of the five biomes. Pillars,
  furniture and alcoves are fine as long as the main lanes stay 3 wide.
- **Doorways: 1 wide.** Every door is a choke point. Rooms should be built so
  holding a door is a real tactic.
- **Wanted, not drawn yet:** a barracks hall with small cubbies off it, each
  cubby its own little bunk room. Rooms that are a hall plus side pockets in
  general, so the 1-wide door pattern repeats inside the room.

## Where the current rooms stand (2026-09-22)

- The two corridor rooms and every maze piece are 1 wide. Against this brief
  they should be redrawn at 3 wide.
- Normal rooms average 30 floor tiles, the smallest of the five biomes. They
  need to grow to be the most open.
- Entrance and Stone Killzone already match the brief.
