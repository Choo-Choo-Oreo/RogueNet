# Cave: design brief

Natural rock. Good size as it is: keep drawing rooms the way the current set
is drawn.

- **Corridors: tight.** Second tightest, behind flesh. Tunnels are mostly 2
  wide with irregular walls, and squeeze to 1 wide in places.
- **Rooms: the least open of the five biomes.** Caves are not rooms so much as
  wider bits of tunnel. Irregular outlines, few straight edges, pockets and
  pillars rather than open floors.
- **Doorways: 1 wide.**

## Where the current rooms stand (2026-09-22)

- Matches the brief. Normal rooms run 40 to 120 floor tiles and the maze pieces
  are 2-wide tunnels.
- The only open question is length: `defines.json` asks for 30 to 50 rooms a
  dive, and most of them are large maze pieces.
